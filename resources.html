Getting Started

What to Expect at Your First Visit

Your first visit gives us an opportunity to learn about your hearing concerns, communication needs and the situations in which you are having difficulty hearing.

We believe hearing care should be personal. We'll take the time to discuss your concerns and determine appropriate next steps based on your individual needs.

  • Discuss your hearing concerns and communication difficulties
  • Review relevant hearing history
  • Complete appropriate hearing evaluation procedures
  • Review and discuss your hearing results
  • Discuss recommendations and possible next steps
  • Ask questions about hearing aids or hearing care
Before Your Appointment

Preparing for a Hearing Evaluation

You don't need to do anything complicated before your hearing evaluation. However, thinking about the situations where you experience the most difficulty can help us better understand your communication needs.

  • Think about where you have the most trouble hearing, such as conversations, television, restaurants or group settings.
  • Bring your current hearing aids if you already wear them.
  • Bring any questions you have about your hearing or hearing aids.
  • Consider bringing a family member or someone familiar with your hearing concerns if you would find that helpful.
Adjusting to Better Hearing

What to Expect With New Hearing Aids

Hearing aids introduce sounds that you may not have heard clearly for some time. Becoming comfortable with those sounds can take time, and your experience may change as you become accustomed to wearing your hearing aids.

Your Own Voice May Sound Different

Your voice may initially sound louder or different than you remember. This often becomes more natural as you adjust.

Everyday Sounds May Be More Noticeable

Sounds such as footsteps, paper, dishes or running water may seem more noticeable when you first begin wearing hearing aids.

Adjustment Takes Time

Your brain needs time to become reacquainted with sounds that may have been reduced by hearing loss.

Follow-Up Is Important

Programming adjustments can help improve comfort and performance as we learn more about your real-world listening experiences.

Protect Your Investment

Hearing Aid Care & Maintenance

Routine care can help keep your hearing aids working properly. Your specific hearing aids may require additional maintenance depending on the style and manufacturer.

Keep Them Clean

Wipe your hearing aids regularly with an appropriate dry, soft cloth and keep microphones and openings clear of debris.

Check Wax Guards & Domes

Wax guards, domes and other replaceable parts may need periodic replacement to maintain proper performance.

Protect From Moisture

Follow the manufacturer's recommendations for protecting your hearing aids from excessive moisture and heat.

Recharge or Replace Batteries

Keep rechargeable hearing aids charged according to the manufacturer's instructions or replace disposable batteries as needed.

Store Them Safely

When not being worn, keep hearing aids in a safe location away from children and pets.

Schedule Professional Maintenance

Periodic professional cleaning and maintenance can help identify issues that may not be obvious during routine home care.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know if I need a hearing evaluation?

If you're having difficulty understanding conversations, frequently asking people to repeat themselves, increasing the television volume or noticing changes in your hearing, a hearing evaluation can help provide more information about your hearing.

Do you work with more than one hearing aid brand?

Yes. Dann Hearing Center is an independent hearing healthcare practice and works with hearing technology from multiple leading manufacturers, including Oticon, ReSound, Phonak, Starkey, Widex and Signia.

Can hearing aids be adjusted after they are fitted?

Yes. Hearing aid programming can be adjusted based on your hearing needs, comfort and experiences in everyday listening situations.

Do hearing aids require maintenance?

Yes. Routine cleaning and maintenance can help hearing aids perform properly. Some components, such as wax guards, domes or receivers, may require periodic attention or replacement.

Can you help with hearing aids purchased somewhere else?

Depending on the manufacturer and model, Dann Hearing Center may be able to assist with hearing aids purchased from another provider or online. Services may include programming, adjustments, cleaning, maintenance or troubleshooting. Contact us to discuss your hearing aids and the service you need.

Do you offer custom earmolds and hearing protection?

Yes. Custom earmolds and hearing protection are available for a variety of individual needs. Contact us to discuss which option may be appropriate for you.

When Should You Contact Us?

Contact Dann Hearing Center if your hearing aids stop working, sound weak or distorted, become uncomfortable, need cleaning or repair, or if you're having difficulty hearing in situations that were previously comfortable. We're also available when you have questions about your devices or believe an adjustment may be helpful.
