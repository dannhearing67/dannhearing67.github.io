Personalized Hearing Care

Hearing Care Services

Personalized service and ongoing hearing care for every stage of your hearing journey.

Hearing Care Centered Around You

At Dann Hearing Center, we take the time to understand your individual hearing needs, communication goals and lifestyle. From hearing evaluations and hearing aid fittings to adjustments, repairs and ongoing care, our goal is to provide personal attention and dependable service.

Hearing Evaluations

Comprehensive hearing evaluations to better understand your hearing needs and help determine appropriate next steps.

Hearing Aid Consultation & Selection

Personalized guidance in selecting hearing technology based on your hearing needs, communication goals, lifestyle, preferences and budget.

Hearing Aid Fittings

Professional hearing aid fitting and orientation designed to help you become comfortable and confident with your hearing devices.

Programming & Adjustments

Personalized hearing aid programming and adjustments to help improve comfort, clarity and overall listening performance.

Cleaning & Maintenance

Routine hearing aid cleaning and maintenance to help keep your devices working properly and performing their best.

Hearing Aid Repairs

Troubleshooting and repair assistance for many hearing aid makes and models.

Custom Earmolds & Hearing Protection

Custom-fit earmolds and hearing protection designed for comfort, secure fit and individual listening or hearing protection needs.

Ongoing Hearing Care

Follow-up appointments, support and personalized care as your hearing needs and listening situations change over time.

Need Help With Your Hearing Aids?

Hearing Aids Purchased Elsewhere

If you purchased hearing aids from another provider or online, Dann Hearing Center may be able to assist with programming, adjustments, cleaning, maintenance or troubleshooting depending on the hearing aid manufacturer and model. Contact us to discuss your devices and the services you need.

Care That Continues After Your Fitting

Successful hearing care doesn't end when you receive your hearing aids. We provide follow-up care, adjustments, maintenance and ongoing support to help you get the most from your hearing technology.
