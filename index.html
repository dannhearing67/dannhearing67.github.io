Welcome to Dann Hearing Center

Personalized Hearing Care Since 1967

At Dann Hearing Center, we believe better hearing begins with listening to you. As an independent hearing healthcare practice, we take the time to understand your hearing needs, lifestyle, communication goals and budget before discussing possible hearing solutions.

Our commitment is to provide personal attention, expert guidance and ongoing support to help you stay connected with the people and sounds that matter most.
