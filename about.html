About Dann Hearing Center

For nearly six decades, Dann Hearing Center has been dedicated to helping individuals reconnect with the sounds that matter most. Since opening our doors in 1967, we have built our reputation on compassionate care, personalized service, and a commitment to improving the quality of life for people with hearing loss throughout Fairfield County.

As an independent hearing healthcare practice, we take the time to understand each patient's unique hearing needs, lifestyle, and budget. Rather than promoting a single manufacturer, we offer hearing solutions from many of the industry's most trusted brands, allowing us to recommend the technology that best fits each individual.

At Dann Hearing Center, we believe that better hearing is about more than hearing aids—it's about restoring confidence, strengthening relationships, and helping people stay connected with family, friends, and the world around them.

Generations of families have trusted Dann Hearing Center because of our knowledgeable recommendations, personalized attention, and dedication to exceptional patient care. As hearing technology continues to advance, our commitment remains the same: to provide expert guidance, outstanding service, and hearing solutions that help our patients enjoy life to the fullest.
