Options for Different Hearing Needs

Hearing Aid Styles

Hearing aids are available in a variety of styles and sizes. The appropriate style depends on factors such as your hearing loss, ear anatomy, comfort, dexterity, listening needs and personal preferences.

Receiver-in-Canal (RIC)

A popular style with a small device worn behind the ear and a thin wire connecting to a receiver positioned in the ear. RIC hearing aids combine discreet design with flexible fitting options.

Behind-the-Ear (BTE)

Worn behind the ear and coupled to the ear with an earmold or other earpiece. BTE hearing aids can accommodate a wide range of hearing needs and offer durable, flexible options.

Custom Hearing Aids

Custom hearing aids are made to fit the individual shape of your ear and are available in several sizes and styles, depending on your hearing needs and preferences.
